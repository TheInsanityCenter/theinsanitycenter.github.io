What I'm Building

"I wanted a full plantation for over 20 years now."

The center originally started as an idea for somewhere for teens and young adults to hang out, a safe zone.

I had been and almost bought a 21 bedroom 16 bath mansion resort. A hangout center full of things like a shop, farm, craft bar, arcade, restaurant/buffet.

I even spent years trying to have fundraisers for it. Total, honestly, $48... They opened a gym that had kid friendly activities and in the long run just went back out of business themselves.

I plan on having my full plantation one day still. I do plan on having parties, raves, concerts, picnics, and other family friendly open to the public events as well either way. So.... There's that too.
