Booking & Deposits ▸

Every appointment needs an advance reservation. No reservation, no appointment. Simple as that.

Booking deposit by appointment category

  • Basic consultations$5
  • Standard services$10
  • Extended appointments$15
  • Specialized / preparation-intensive services$20

Which tier is your appointment? Here's the plain map:

  • Basic ($5): the sit-down stuff. Consultations, inquiries, resource referrals.
  • Standard ($10): workshops, studio time, sessions, most hourly services.
  • Extended ($15): parties, photography shoots, event rentals, day-rate bookings.
  • Specialized ($20): anything we build custom for you, or anything needing prep before you arrive.
  • Deposits are credited toward the final service price. They are not additional service charges.
  • Remaining balances are due according to the appointment agreement.
  • Cancellation, rescheduling, and refund terms get disclosed before any money changes hands. No surprises after the fact.
  • Special events, accommodations, and facility rentals may carry separate booking terms. Those get spelled out when you book.

Daily capacity during launch: five scheduled appointments per day. That's it, five. Additional appointments may open up as staffing, facilities, and operating capacity expand. I'd rather run a clean, unhurried house than cram people in like a waiting room.

Ages & Access Policy ▸

Ages matter here more than they do at most places, because this compound runs two different worlds on the same land. Here is exactly how age works at the Center, so nobody gets surprised at the door.

The two sides

  • Public Grounds: all ages. Families, kids, grandparents, everybody. Kids under 18 stay with a parent or guardian, same as you'd expect anywhere families gather.
  • Private Clubhouse: 21+ only. Valid government photo ID, verified in person, at first visit. No ID = no entry, no exceptions, no "but my friend vouches for me."

By service

  • All Ages: public events, nature and agriculture, family hours for wellness and recreation, standard creative and studio work, housing stays.
  • 18+ to book or contract: housing applications, wellness bookings, support services, workshop registration. Ages 13–17 can join most workshops with a parent or guardian signed on. Hot work like welding is 18+, no minors at the torch.
The short version: all ages on the public side, 21+ with ID behind the private door, 18+ to book most services, and no exceptions to any of it.
