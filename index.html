What Fantasy Means To Me

Fantasy to me is not about escaping reality. It's about imagination, wonder, old-fashioned fun, Victorian whimsy, storytelling, dressing up, penny carnivals, picnics, games, and making a place where kids and adults can play pretend together.

It's cozy, magical, theatrical, and community-based. It is NOT horror, NOT gore, NOT explicit adult content.

Bring Us Your Fantasy

Here's my favorite part of the whole guide. Everything listed above is what exists right now. The Center is not finished, and it isn't supposed to be. If the thing you want isn't on the list, that is not a no. That's a conversation.

Someone wants to build a working robot. Someone wants one single day of blacksmithing. Someone wants to make a movie with zero equipment. Someone wants to try on a whole new identity in a costume nobody at home would understand. Someone wants to build a tiny house, learn to ride a horse, or take old electronics apart in a controlled space just to see what's actually inside. And somebody out there just wants somewhere to explore an interest without having to justify it to anybody.

That's what "Bring Us Your Fantasy" is for. You send the idea, and we figure out together whether it can be made possible, what it needs, and who else would want in. Not every idea will be feasible, affordable, or safe. But the starting point here is curiosity, not "that's weird."

The Bring Us Your Fantasy form ▸

Here's the whole form, right out in the open, so you know exactly what we'd ask of you:

  1. Your name / scene name + age (18+ to submit. Private-sector ideas: 21+ vetted members only.)
  2. The fantasy, in your own words. What happens? What does it look like?
  3. Solo thing, group thing, or an event for the whole grounds?
  4. Which side: public (all ages) or private (21+ members)?
  5. Rough budget in mind, even if the honest answer is "none yet"
  6. Gear, space, or skills you're bringing to the table?
  7. Safety concerns you've already thought about?
  8. Timeline: one afternoon, a weekend, or something recurring?
Then email it to the Center.
