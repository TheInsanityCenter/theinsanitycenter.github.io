The Insanity Center / The Insanity Plantation

What I'm Building

"I wanted a full plantation for over 20 years now."

The center originally started as an idea for somewhere for teens and young adults to hang out, a safe zone.

I had been and almost bought a 21 bedroom 16 bath mansion resort. A hangout center full of things like a shop, farm, craft bar, arcade, restaurant/buffet.

I even spent years trying to have fundraisers for it. Total, honestly, $48... They opened a gym that had kid friendly activities and in the long run just went back out of business themselves.

I plan on having my full plantation one day still. I do plan on having parties, raves, concerts, picnics, and other family friendly open to the public events as well either way. So.... There's that too.

What Fantasy Means To Me

Fantasy to me is not about escaping reality. It's about imagination, wonder, old-fashioned fun, Victorian whimsy, storytelling, dressing up, penny carnivals, picnics, games, and making a place where kids and adults can play pretend together.

It's cozy, magical, theatrical, and community-based. It is NOT horror, NOT gore, NOT explicit adult content.

  • Play

  • Old-Fashioned Fun

Bring Us Your Fantasy

Here's my favorite part of the whole guide. Everything listed above is what exists right now. The Center is not finished, and it isn't supposed to be. If the thing you want isn't on the list, that is not a no. That's a conversation.

Someone wants to build a working robot. Someone wants one single day of blacksmithing. Someone wants to make a movie with zero equipment. Someone wants to try on a whole new identity in a costume nobody at home would understand. Someone wants to build a tiny house, learn to ride a horse, or take old electronics apart in a controlled space just to see what's actually inside. And somebody out there just wants somewhere to explore an interest without having to justify it to anybody.

That's what "Bring Us Your Fantasy" is for. You send the idea, and we figure out together whether it can be made possible, what it needs, and who else would want in. Not every idea will be feasible, affordable, or safe. But the starting point here is curiosity, not "that's weird."

The Bring Us Your Fantasy form ▸

Here's the whole form, right out in the open, so you know exactly what we'd ask of you:

  1. Your name / scene name + age (18+ to submit. Private-sector ideas: 21+ vetted members only.)
  2. The fantasy, in your own words. What happens? What does it look like?
  3. Solo thing, group thing, or an event for the whole grounds?
  4. Which side: public (all ages) or private (21+ members)?
  5. Rough budget in mind, even if the honest answer is "none yet"
  6. Gear, space, or skills you're bringing to the table?
  7. Safety concerns you've already thought about?
  8. Timeline: one afternoon, a weekend, or something recurring?
Then email it to the Center.

Booking & Deposits ▸

Every appointment needs an advance reservation. No reservation, no appointment. Simple as that.

Booking deposit by appointment category

  • Basic consultations$5
  • Standard services$10
  • Extended appointments$15
  • Specialized / preparation-intensive services$20

Which tier is your appointment? Here's the plain map:

  • Basic ($5): the sit-down stuff. Consultations, inquiries, resource referrals.
  • Standard ($10): workshops, studio time, sessions, most hourly services.
  • Extended ($15): parties, photography shoots, event rentals, day-rate bookings.
  • Specialized ($20): anything we build custom for you, or anything needing prep before you arrive.
  • Deposits are credited toward the final service price. They are not additional service charges.
  • Remaining balances are due according to the appointment agreement.
  • Cancellation, rescheduling, and refund terms get disclosed before any money changes hands. No surprises after the fact.
  • Special events, accommodations, and facility rentals may carry separate booking terms. Those get spelled out when you book.

Daily capacity during launch: five scheduled appointments per day. That's it, five. Additional appointments may open up as staffing, facilities, and operating capacity expand. I'd rather run a clean, unhurried house than cram people in like a waiting room.

Ages & Access Policy ▸

Ages matter here more than they do at most places, because this compound runs two different worlds on the same land. Here is exactly how age works at the Center, so nobody gets surprised at the door.

The two sides

  • Public Grounds: all ages. Families, kids, grandparents, everybody. Kids under 18 stay with a parent or guardian, same as you'd expect anywhere families gather.
  • Private Clubhouse: 21+ only. Valid government photo ID, verified in person, at first visit. No ID = no entry, no exceptions, no "but my friend vouches for me."

By service

  • All Ages: public events, nature and agriculture, family hours for wellness and recreation, standard creative and studio work, housing stays.
  • 18+ to book or contract: housing applications, wellness bookings, support services, workshop registration. Ages 13–17 can join most workshops with a parent or guardian signed on. Hot work like welding is 18+, no minors at the torch.
The short version: all ages on the public side, 21+ with ID behind the private door, 18+ to book most services, and no exceptions to any of it.

Drop a Dime - $1 and up

This is a public fund to open a private, consent-first, 21+ social club (with family friendly events and common areas) in Carbondale / Scranton, PA. The Insanity Center is coming to NEPA - if you want it here.

This is an open donation fund to help open the center. This is not members-only. This is not an investment.

Why a dime?

It costs a dime ($0.10) for my time - that's the base. No cap. If you want to drop a million toward the venture, you are free to do so. One button. You put in the amount you want to offer.

Initial capital and funding is needed until enough memberships exist to cover expenses. I don't want costs and expenses to fall upon any person or members.

I want it opened via public desire for such a place to exist in this location. Kinda like one would sign a petition - the people pay $0.10 to say "hey, I'm all for this to be a thing here."

Your dime is your signature. Your extra is what builds it.

Where your drop goes

Lease and security, insurance, safety equipment, cleaning and hygiene build-out, privacy and sound setup for The Insanity Center LLC. All funds go directly to opening costs.

This is not equity, not profit-share, not pay-for-play. This is community funding to get the doors open debt-free so members aren't carrying the burden.

I'm not asking you to fund a mansion. I'm asking for a dollar toward Phase Zero - the search. If you have a space, or you've wanted to build this too - message me. If you can't help with space, $1 toward the search means everything.
