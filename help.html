The Insanity Center Donation Fund

$0.10

The dime is the idea. The button starts at a buck - after that, you put in whatever you want.

This is a public fund to open a private, consent-first, 21+ social club (with family friendly events and common areas) in Carbondale / Scranton, PA. The Insanity Center is coming to NEPA - if you want it here.

This is an open donation fund to help open the center. This is not members-only. This is not an investment.

Why a dime?

It costs a dime ($0.10) for my time - that's the base. No cap. If you want to drop a million toward the venture, you are free to do so. One button. You put in the amount you want to offer.

Initial capital and funding is needed until enough memberships exist to cover expenses. I don't want costs and expenses to fall upon any person or members.

I want it opened via public desire for such a place to exist in this location. Kinda like one would sign a petition - the people pay $0.10 to say "hey, I'm all for this to be a thing here."

Your dime is your signature. Your extra is what builds it.

Where your drop goes

Lease and security, insurance, safety equipment, cleaning and hygiene build-out, privacy and sound setup for The Insanity Center LLC. All funds go directly to opening costs.

This is not equity, not profit-share, not pay-for-play. This is community funding to get the doors open debt-free so members aren't carrying the burden.

I'm not asking you to fund a mansion. I'm asking for a dollar toward Phase Zero - the search. If you have a space, or you've wanted to build this too - message me. If you can't help with space, $1 toward the search means everything.

Drop a Dime - $1 and up

Thank you for your time and your desire to have this here. - Joy
